**Key Technical Details & Innovations:**
- **GPU-Accelerated 2D Block Matching:** Sub-pixel displacement tracking operating at $>120$ FPS.
- **De-aliasing & Regularization:** Built-in spatial-continuity penalties to reduce out-of-plane speckle decorrelation.
- **Clinical Metrics:** Direct automated computation of Global Longitudinal Strain (GLS) and regional myocardial work indices.
