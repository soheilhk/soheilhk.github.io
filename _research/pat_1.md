**Key Technical Details & Innovations:**
- **Spatial Resolution Zoning:** Closed-form mathematical mapping partitioning the circular imaging chamber into distinct lateral/axial resolution zones based on focal curvature (positive/negative focal lengths).
- **Acoustic Blind Spot Characterization:** Analytical formulation of blind spot angular distributions for arbitrary point acoustic sources distributed across the full field-of-view (FOV).
- **System Design & Optimization Framework:** Provides a parametric forward-design criterion to either predict the optimal reconstruction region for existing hardware or back-calculate transducer acoustic parameters required to meet target Point Spread Function (PSF) tolerances.
