### Project Overview
Quantification of regional myocardial mechanics via speckle tracking echocardiography (STE) provides sensitive diagnostic metrics prior to manifest ejection fraction degradation.

### Methodology & Results
- **GPU Acceleration:** High-throughput block-matching algorithm optimized on NVIDIA Tensor Cores.
- **Frame Rate:** Achieved global longitudinal strain (GLS) and radial strain tensors calculation at **>120 FPS**.
- **Artifact Reduction:** Elastographic quality metrics to eliminate decorrelated out-of-plane tracking artifacts.
