**Key Technical Details & Innovations:**
- **Compound Orbital–Axial Scanning:** Dual-axis acquisition scheme in which a single-element transducer both orbits the region of interest (ROI) and rotates around its own center at analytically derived angles.
- **Physics-Driven Rotation Synthesis:** Central rotation angles computed directly from transducer central frequency, aperture diameter, and ROI radius—synthesizing a virtual detector with wide acceptance angle.
- **Location-Independent Tangential Resolution:** Eliminates aperture-induced tangential resolution degradation at off-center positions using only 150 spatial samples and ±8°/±16° central rotations.
- **Validated Across Simulation & Experiment:** Quantitative PSF evaluation with point absorbers, vasculature phantom assessment, plus experimental validation using 0.5 mm pencil leads and a leaf skeleton phantom.
