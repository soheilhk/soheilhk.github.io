PACT Reconstruction 2024 – Present

Sparse-Sampling & Coherence Artifact Suppression

High-channel data acquisition in ring-array PACT is computationally prohibitive and hardware-intensive. We developed a robust spatio-temporal coherence and stability-based weighting framework to suppress grating lobes and streak artifacts under severe angular sub-sampling (up to 4×–8× element reduction) while preserving subtle microvascular fidelity.

The method leverages complex-signal cross-correlations across sub-apertures, providing an order-of-magnitude improvement in contrast-to-noise ratio (CNR) without iterative forward-model optimization bottlenecks.

Systems & HPC
CUDA / High-Performance Computing 2023 – 2024

Real-Time Universal Back-Projection Engine on Heterogeneous GPUs

Volumetric 3D and high-frame-rate 2D PACT require extreme data throughput. We designed a modular C++/CUDA-based Universal Back-Projection (UBP) and Delay-and-Sum pipeline optimized for arbitrary sensor geometries (planar, cylindrical, and spherical cups).

Integrated with a Qt/QML frontend, this engine achieves sustained 60+ FPS reconstruction for 512-channel real-time functional vascular imaging and hemodynamic monitoring.

Deep Learning
Physics-Guided Neural Nets 2022 – 2023

Physics-Guided Deep Learning for Low-Fluence Laser Imaging

Safe in vivo clinical translation requires operating well beneath ANSI laser safety limits, yielding raw RF signals buried in acoustic noise. We implemented a physics-informed unrolled neural network that combines learned acoustic propagation priors with deep residual denoising.

Evaluated across in vivo rat brain cortex and phantom datasets, the model recovers fine capillary structures at laser fluence levels reduced by over 70%.
