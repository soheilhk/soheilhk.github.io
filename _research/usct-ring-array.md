**Key Technical Details & Innovations:**
- **Sparse Synthetic Transmit Aperture (STA):** Optimized element excitation sequences reducing acquisition time while preserving isotropic lateral resolution.
- **Speed-of-Sound Heterogeneity Correction:** Ray-tracing and time-of-flight (TOF) refraction correction across heterogeneous acoustic pathways.
- **Artifact Suppression:** Adaptive coherence factor (CF) weighting to eliminate spatial grating lobes and acoustic clutter in cylindrical geometries.
