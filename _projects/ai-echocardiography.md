### System Overview
Addressing inter-observer variability by embedding deep-learning and speckle-tracking intelligence directly into the echocardiography review workflow.

### Key Technical Contributions
*   **Automated Segmentation:** Integrated neural network architectures (image-to-image) for real-time identification of cardiac views and boundary segmentation of LV, RV, and LA chambers.
*   **Deformation Imaging:** Developed robust 2D speckle-tracking algorithms for precise myocardial deformation analysis.
*   **Clinical Quantification:** 
    *   Automated biplane Simpson’s rule for Ejection Fraction (EF) and volumetric estimations.
    *   Real-time strain/strain-rate quantification.
*   **Standardization:** Automated generation of ASE/EACVI-compliant diagnostic reports, including 17-segment Bullseye polar plots for standardized clinical assessment.
