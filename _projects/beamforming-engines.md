### System Overview
Solving computational bottlenecks to deliver real-time, high-quality image reconstruction on commercial hardware.

### Key Technical Contributions
*   **Scalability:** Architected and validated digital beamforming pipelines optimized for 64-channel to 256-channel hardware topologies.
*   **Accelerator Integration:** Developed hybrid FPGA/GPU (CUDA) architectures for real-time synthetic transmit aperture and plane-wave compounding.
*   **Reconstruction Algorithms:** Implemented and optimized non-linear adaptive beamforming techniques:
    *   **Minimum Variance (MV)** for improved contrast.
    *   **Delay-Multiply-and-Sum (DMAS)** for spatial resolution enhancement.
    *   **Short-Lag Spatial Coherence (SLSC)** for clutter suppression.
