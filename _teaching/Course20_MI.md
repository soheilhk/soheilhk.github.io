Key Takeaway
======
This course treats modern medical imaging not merely as a diagnostic tool, but as a suite of inverse problems in linear physics and spatial signal processing. It is ideal for students or R&D engineers who wish to understand the exact mathematical link between the physical physics of energy-tissue interactions and the final spatial resolution, contrast, and noise properties of reconstructed images.
