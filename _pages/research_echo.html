Since 2020, my work in echocardiography has bridged advanced acoustic beamforming theory with end-to-end medical device engineering through the collaborative design and deployment of a complete clinical-grade echocardiography machine. My contributions span the entire signal processing and software pipeline—from developing adaptive beamforming architectures and specialized clutter-rejection algorithms to implementing high-throughput, GPU-accelerated reconstruction engines and clinical UI frameworks. To address challenging cardiac acoustic windows, I have introduced correlation- and stability-guided beamforming methods (such as SG-PWC) that effectively suppress reverberation and clutter while enhancing endocardial boundary definition and myocardial contrast. Together, these efforts translate physics-driven signal processing into a robust, real-time diagnostic platform for dynamic cardiac imaging.
