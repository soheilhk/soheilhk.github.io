Since 2019, during my master’s and PhD studies, I have focused on photoacoustic tomography, primarily using ring-array geometries and, in some projects, linear arrays. My research has addressed image-quality limitations caused by sparse sampling, finite-aperture effects, limited-view acquisition, and bandwidth constraints. I have developed reconstruction and signal-processing methods to suppress artifacts, improve spatial resolution, and preserve the structure of photoacoustic absorbers, with the broader goal of enabling faster and more reliable PAT imaging.
