My industry portfolio represents a multi-faceted approach to ultrasound innovation, characterized by a series of collaborative endeavors at the forefront of medical imaging. Working alongside Prof. Kavehvash, Prof. Mostafavi, and Prof. Nili, I have contributed to a range of high-impact projects centered on advancing ultrasound technology through rigorous teamwork and interdisciplinary expertise. Our collective efforts have spanned three primary domains: the architecture of comprehensive, real-time clinical ultrasound software suites; the development of high-performance GPU and FPGA-accelerated beamforming engines; and the integration of AI-driven deep learning for automated echocardiographic quantification. These projects, executed as a unified team, reflect a shared commitment to enhancing diagnostic accuracy, processing efficiency, and clinical utility in modern ultrasound imaging.
