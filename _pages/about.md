## Research
### Ultrasound Imaging

Advanced ultrasound beamforming, GPU-accelerated imaging, Doppler imaging, raw channel-data processing, and real-time ultrasound system development.

### Photoacoustic Imaging

Photoacoustic tomography, sparse-array acquisition, computational reconstruction, detector-subset methods, and artifact suppression.

### Computational Imaging & Tomography

Ultrasound computed tomography, sparse and circular-array imaging, image reconstruction, computational methods, and artifact reduction.

### AI for Medical Imaging

Artificial intelligence and deep learning for ultrasound and echocardiography, including image classification, segmentation, speckle tracking, and automated image analysis.
