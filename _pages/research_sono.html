Since 2022, expanding on our ultrasound system architecture, I have contributed to developing specialized imaging pipelines and clinical presets for carotid vascular, abdominal, and gynecological (GYN) sonography. This work focuses on optimizing multi-frequency transmit-receive schemes, adaptive dynamic range control, and custom beamforming strategies tailored to distinct anatomical depths and acoustic impedance profiles—ranging from fine intima-media thickness (IMT) boundary resolution in high-frequency superficial carotid scans to deep-tissue acoustic penetration and speckle reduction in abdominal and pelvic imaging. Across these modalities, I implemented GPU-accelerated B-mode filtering, edge-preserving clutter suppression, and real-time scan-conversion engines, ensuring crisp organ margin delineation, enhanced lesion-to-tissue contrast, and robust diagnostic image quality across diverse clinical workflows.
