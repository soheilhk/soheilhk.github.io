Since 2023, building upon my extensive background in ultrasound imaging and ring-array geometries during the final stages of my PhD, I expanded my research into Reflection Ultrasound Computed Tomography (RUCT). This research focuses on advancing synthetic aperture beamforming and reconstruction frameworks tailored to full-aperture circular arrays. To enable high-frame-rate cross-sectional imaging without sacrificing acoustic image quality, I developed physics-based transmit sparsification criteria alongside effective apodization strategies to control diffraction effects and side-lobe interference. Furthermore, I investigated phased-array transmit compounding to enhance acoustic penetration and signal fidelity, as well as a Transmit Data-Subset Stability (DSS) framework that suppresses sparse-sampling streaks and grating clutter by evaluating inter-subset acoustic consistency. Together, these works establish an efficient, hardware-compatible beamforming pipeline for fast, high-resolution reflection USCT.
